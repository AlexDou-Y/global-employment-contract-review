# Source Verification Rules

## Source Hierarchy

Use the highest available source. Prefer current official sources.

1. Official legislation, labor code, employment act, gazette, consolidated statute, or government legal database
2. Official labor ministry, immigration authority, tax authority, social security authority, regulator, or court/labor tribunal guidance
3. Official government FAQ, employer guide, wage order, leave entitlement page, or visa/work permit page
4. EOR/vendor/local counsel material only as secondary support
5. Reputable law firm or accounting firm updates only when official sources are unavailable or too general
6. Model knowledge only as fallback

## Citation Requirements

For every risk finding, include:

- source name;
- link or document title if available;
- relevant section/article/page if available;
- access date when using web sources;
- whether the source is official, vendor, law firm, or model fallback.

Use concise citations. Do not paste long copyrighted text.

## Source Support Test

Before citing a source, verify that it supports the exact judgment being made:

| Check | Required standard |
|---|---|
| Topic match | The source discusses the same legal topic, not only the same country |
| Rule match | The source supports the specific rule, threshold, entitlement, timing, or obligation stated |
| Jurisdiction match | The source applies to the country/region and, where relevant, state/province/city being reviewed |
| Currentness | The source appears current or is an official consolidated source |
| Authority | The source type is correctly labelled as official, vendor, law firm, professional institution, or fallback |

If the source is relevant but does not prove the exact point, write the point as a confirmation item rather than a final finding.

## Official Source Missing

If no official source is found, write exactly:

`⚠️ 未检索到官方来源，以下基于模型知识库，需人工核实。`

Then:

- mark the finding as at least 🟠 if it could affect legality, cost, visa, tax, social security, termination, or contract validity;
- list the party who should confirm: Legal, local counsel, EOR provider, visa vendor, Tax/Finance, payroll vendor, or business owner;
- avoid definitive wording such as "must", "illegal", or "compliant" unless backed by a reliable source.

## Search Guidance

When online search is available and the point is jurisdiction-specific or may have changed, verify before finalizing. Search in English and local language when useful:

- `[country] employment contract mandatory terms official`
- `[country] labor code employment contract written particulars`
- `[country] probation period employment contract official`
- `[country] annual leave sick leave labor law official`
- `[country] termination notice severance employment official`
- `[country] work permit employer obligations official`
- local-language keywords for labor contract, wages, working time, leave, termination, social insurance, non-compete

## Source Quality Warnings

Do not rely solely on:

- old blog posts without date;
- AI-generated summaries;
- vendor marketing pages;
- country guides that do not cite law;
- non-official translations when local-language law is controlling.

Do not treat a source as official unless it is published by a government, regulator, court, tribunal, official legal database, or other official authority.
